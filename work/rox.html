Overview

The brief

National Day is the most crowded moment of the Saudi advertising year. ROX needed to join the celebration with real pride — built on the official «عِزّنا بطبعنا» identity — while keeping its SUV the hero of every frame.

01

The challenge

Stand out among hundreds of National Day ads, stay true to the official identity, and adapt one idea to very different sizes — tall roll-ups, near-square posts, ultra-wide banners and 2:1 billboards.

02

The approach

Riyadh’s icons — Kingdom Centre, Al Faisaliah and the twisting towers — wrapped in Sadu and Al-Qatt patterns, a heritage fort, Diriyah’s mud walls and the open desert. A family of lines grows from the theme: عزّنا بأصالتنا، بهمّتنا، برؤيتنا، بجودنا.

03

The result

A flexible campaign system: one visual language, many headlines, and layouts that keep the logo, the theme lock-up and the car perfectly balanced in every format.
