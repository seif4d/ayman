Overview

The brief

Price-led offers usually end up looking cheap. For SUTRA’s 999 promotion the goal was the opposite: keep the price loud and clear, but wrap it in imagery that celebrates youth culture and the brand’s attitude — Move Different.

01

The challenge

Run one simple, nationwide offer across many posts without repetition — and without the price swallowing the fashion.

02

The approach

Three cinematic worlds: Abbey Road–inspired urban crossings, a retro room of cathode-ray TVs flashing the offer, and moody street photography with classic cars.

03

The result

A high-energy campaign where the 999 price becomes part of the art direction — consistent, bold and unmistakably SUTRA.
