Overview

The brief

A brand lives in the hands of the people who meet it: the business card passed at a stand, the notebook in a meeting, the mug on a client’s desk. The goal was to carry Luxe’s identity across all of them — consistent, premium and instantly recognisable.

01

The challenge

Keep one premium identity intact across very different materials — paper, leather, metal, glass and ceramic — and very different sizes, from a keychain to an A4 envelope.

02

The approach

A strict system: a deep-purple base, a rose-gold accent used for trims, rings and the X stroke, and a clean white wordmark. The X works alone as a monogram, as a subtle watermark pattern on envelopes, and the script tagline signs off the cards and notebook.

03

The result

A cohesive kit — business cards, notebook, envelopes, pen, mug, bottle, USB, card holder, desk organiser, keychain, mouse pad and more — that looks like one family wherever it lands.
