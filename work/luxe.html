Overview

The brief

Luxe designs and builds exhibition stands, events and interiors — work that doesn’t exist yet when a client is deciding. The campaign had to make that promise, Turning Fantasy into Reality, feel concrete, premium and unmistakably Luxe.

01

The challenge

Show the value of spaces that haven’t been built yet, and explain a broad offer — stand design, fabrication, event production and interior fit-out — without turning the feed into a service list.

02

The approach

Turned the X from the Luxe logo into the campaign’s hero object: an X-shaped meeting table, a monumental X in the desert, an X platform holding three worlds. Visual metaphors — an ESC key, a puzzle piece, a signpost, six stacked steps — carry the rest of the story.

03

The result

A recognisable campaign system: one purple-to-peach palette, bold headlines paired with a handwritten accent, and a single brand symbol tying every post together.
