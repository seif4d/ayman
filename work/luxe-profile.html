Overview

The brief

Luxe sells work that clients can’t see yet — stands, events and interiors that only exist once they’re built. The profile had to make that promise feel real: who Luxe is, what it does, how it works and who already trusts it, in one deck a sales team can send or present.

01

The challenge

Turn a wide offer — exhibitions, event branding, interiors, documentation, audiovisual and giveaways — plus dozens of projects and clients into a story that stays clear from the first slide to the last.

02

The approach

A clear narrative in chapters — intro, services, team & process, projects, clients, contact — with bold divider slides built on the Luxe X, a deep-purple and peach system, glass icons and photo-led project grids.

03

The result

A premium, on-brand profile that works on screen and in the room — each chapter easy to find, every project and client given room to speak.
