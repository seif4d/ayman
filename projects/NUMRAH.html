NUMRAH — Mobile Publishing & Reading Platform

Reimagining the digital reading experience with dark-mode optimized typography, seamless navigation flows, and distraction-free user journeys.

01. Overview & Challenge

The Problem Statement

Digital readers and independent publishers on the NUMRAH platform experienced visual fatigue due to high clutter, unoptimized font hierarchy, and a multi-step navigation flow that resulted in a high bounce rate during long reading sessions.

The Challenge

Users struggled with complex menu navigation and harsh screen brightness during nighttime reading sessions, causing a 35% user drop-off rate within 10 minutes of app usage.

The UX Solution

Architected a high-contrast dark mode UI system with adaptive typography, micro-animations, and a 2-click streamlined navigation bar prioritizing content immersion.

02. Visual Strategy

Design System & Color Palette

To deliver an effortless reading experience, we developed a cohesive dark UI design system using deep charcoal background tones combined with vibrant emerald focus accents.
