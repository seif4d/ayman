About Me

Visual Storyteller. Brand Builder. Creative at heart.

I'm a Mid-Level Graphic Designer based in Cairo, Egypt, with 3+ years turning brand visions into sharp visual realities. My core work covers brand identity, social media creatives, print production, and marketing design — I also bring UI/UX skills to projects that need it. Every piece I create is built to communicate clearly and look unforgettable.
